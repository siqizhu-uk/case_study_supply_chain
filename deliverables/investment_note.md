# Nordic / Logitech / GN: Q3 2026 print previews

*27 September 2026 · Public data only · Model and sources: `README.md`; the brief answered in full: the dashboard's Analysis tab (`deliverables/my_analysis.md`)*

## Bottom line

The guide already contains each company's order book, so we do not try to out-forecast demand. We forecast two things the guide leaves out: management's habitual guide error, and the channel state that the supply chain measures.

- **Nordic (22 Oct): revenue 238m, the top of the 220–240 guide; GM 53.1%.** Nordic beats its guide by 4.2% on average when its distributors are neither building stock nor supply-short, which is today's state. The downside is larger than the upside: if distributors start building stock, 221 (−17). A restocking beat (253) would be borrowed from Q4.
- **Logitech (≈27 Oct): net sales 1,226m, just above the 1,185–1,220 guide; GM 45.3% against ~44% guided.** Its five quarterly guides, pooled with 12 peers', say management guides ~2% low; it has beaten its GM guide 4 times out of 4. Its channel is 1–2 weeks below target with sell-through 4 points ahead of sell-in, which skews risk to the upside (the channel identity gives 1,316). That is not in the point, because the guide already knew that sell-through.
- **GN (5 Nov): revenue 2,247 DKKm (+1.6%), adj. EBITA 13.5%.** GN's August guide has overshot in 4 of 5 years (−3.3 pts on average), so we sit below every alternative (2,348–2,476). On the full-year guide, H2 must earn 13.5% against 11.6% bridged from Q2 (8.9% without a one-off tariff refund): either Q4 is heavy, or the guide is at risk.

## The supply-chain decisions behind the call

**1. Lag: reasoned before any regression.** Edge by edge: Amazon cuts orders to Logitech (~4.5 weeks including weekly replenishment), then Logitech's builds and chip orders reach Nordic (~19.6 weeks including S&OP, MRP and distributor reorders). **A change in Amazon sell-out reaches Nordic's revenue as an order signal after ≈24 weeks (90% 21–27; ≈1.8 quarters); the goods alone take ≈17.** Planning delays are not disclosed; if part of them already sits in inventory cover, the Amazon route is 19–24 weeks. The data fit anywhere from 19.5 to 37 weeks (one cycle). The lag depends on the regime: ~4 quarters in the 2021–22 shortage, ~2 in the destock, each from one turning point. *In the forecast:* component lead times of 12–16 weeks mean Q3's demand was ordered before Nordic guided, so the chain gets weight 0 in the guided quarter. The graph drives Q4 (Logitech's sell-in through the Nordic–ODM–Logitech segment).

**2. Mechanism: channel stock moves revenue, balance sheets do not.** Revenue is sell-in; the gap to sell-through is channel stock. Nordic's own 193 inventory days (a build management attributes to nRF54 wafers; not quote-checked) is not a demand signal. Only Logitech quantifies its channel: its sell-through gap, anchored on "at target" statements (±0.7 weeks), shows 1–2 weeks lean today. Nordic describes its distributors only in words (healthy; some small customers may order ahead). GN Enterprise's sell-out has matched or beaten sell-in for 8 quarters. The amplification sits at the component tier (Nordic Consumer ×2.7 Logitech sell-in). *In the forecast:* the channel enters once, as the state of the quarter before the guide: distributors building stock costs peers −1.64 pts of guide error (t −4.4, 619 firm-quarters) and Nordic −7.4.

**3. Attribution: bracketed, not guessed.** Nordic discloses no customer split, so five independent legs bracket it: a bottom-up FCC teardown census (units × radios × socket share × ASP), Nordic's IFRS 8 customer ceiling, a category prior, a proprietary-radio floor and the 2023 natural experiment. **Logitech + GN ≈ 16% of Nordic revenue (13–20%), ≈ 27% of Nordic Consumer**, if Logitech buys through ODMs; ≈ 11% if Nordic invoices it directly. GN is ≈ $8m. *In the forecast:* weight 0 in the guided quarter, so the share does not move the point. One quarter out Nordic moves ≈ 3× what the share implies, because its other consumer customers ride the same cycle.

**4. Structural breaks: tested by event, the cycle dated by data.** Breaks: the 2021–22 shortage (the Nordic–Logitech link breaks at its end, 2022Q4, p 0.04; excluded from the lag fits), the 2022–24 distributor build and destock (2023: top-10 customers −3%, broad market −46%), and the 2025 US price step (2.8% of Logitech's sales, so dollars stop tracking units; 3 quarters cannot test the link). Post-COVID normalisation moved the input, not the link (p 0.77). The AI / memory cycle breaks only proxies, and not significantly (p 0.15–0.57). Stable: Logitech's customer mix and the lag kernel.

**5. Not built.** No Amazon rank, price or promotion scrapes: no free history to test them (Keepa is paid). Existing sources and libraries instead: SEC XBRL, ECB rates, findchips, FCC filings. Deliberately stopped: the FCC census at 38 of 81 public grants, the factor search after about a dozen candidates.

**6. Limitations.** For the guided quarter nothing beats the guide: lag models err 3.9–5.9× more than guide + habit, and across 12 peers the beat's sd is 3.9% against 29% for revenue growth. The beat is management's forecast error, not demand. Weakest links: the channel *level* (nobody discloses weeks on hand), the invoicing route behind attribution, and a channel state whose thresholds were set after seeing 2020–26 and which flags turns a quarter late. The chain does not help GN (~1% of Nordic).
