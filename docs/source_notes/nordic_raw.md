## Qualitative
- Q3 2026 report date: 22 Oct 2026. Q2 26 consensus was 208.7 rev (9 analysts) vs actual 218.6.
- Q4 21: supply constrained (wafers) through 2022 into Q1 23; Dec-21 price increase applied to distributor inventory.
- Q3 22: significant inventory adjustments at distributors (proprietary). Q1 23: lower deliveries to tier-1s due to inventory adjustments; backlog APM dropped ("not a good demand indicator").
- Q3 23: broad-market customers depleting inventories; Nordic building own inventory. Q4 23: weak end demand + inventory adjustments at end-customers and distributors; 2023 broad-market BLE -46% to 208m, top-10 BLE ~flat (276 vs 285).
- Q1 24 trough: sell-through from distributors significantly higher than 74.5m revenue; distribution inventory adjustments "predominantly behind us"; healthcare -85% from a tier-1 inventory adjustment.
- Q2 24: distributor inventory balanced/normal; individual end customers still excessive into 2025; 10m nRF9160 write-down.
- Q3 25: channel inventory "healthy, a bit on the lighter side"; Future Electronics added as distributor.
- Q1 26: broad-market up ~40% from 2024 lows, still 25% below 2022 peak; customers selling more end product, not restocking.
- Q2 26: distributor inventory unchanged/healthy; CEO: cannot rule out some small customers advancing orders on capacity worries; own inventory build deliberate (nRF54 wafers); test capacity +40-50% in 2026; NWC 26% LTM revenue.
- Distribution share: NCR (Sep 25): ~40% of revenue from broad distribution market; 2023 broad-market BLE 208/484 = 43%.
- Top-10 customer share of BLE/total: 2021 40%; 2022 44%; 2023 57%; 2024 58%; 2025 ~57%; H1 26 ~54%. No customer >10%.
- Logitech/GN never named. NCR names Amazon, Google, Microsoft as major customers. Nordic lists PC peripherals/gaming as consumer applications.
- nRF54: launched Q4 24; limited 2025 revenue; >20% of certified designs Q2 26; "gradually increasing revenue".
- Tariffs: no major effect (Q2 25).
- LT targets: >20% avg growth 2024-30; GM >50%.
