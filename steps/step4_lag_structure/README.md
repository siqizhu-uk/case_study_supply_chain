# Step 4 — Revenue propagation (lag structure)

**Question.** How many quarters from retail sell-out to Nordic revenue? The lag is reasoned first; the data are then read for what they can measure.

`src/lags.py`: the reasoned lag in weeks per tier (`config/model.yaml` `lag_weeks:`, 15 / 22 / 32 weeks) is stated first and stays the documented prior (decision L1; step 5's supply graph is its flow-weighted refinement, ~19 weeks). Then **Nordic consumer revenue vs the downstream / industry cycle: timing** — correlation of Nordic consumer YoY with Logitech radio-category YoY at lags 0–4 (all quarters and by regime), turning-point alignment and amplitude ratios (bullwhip).

How to read the timing (decision L2): the driver is Logitech **sell-in**; the target is **all** consumer customers, of which Logitech + GN are ~16% of Nordic revenue (step 2); common-cycle controls outside the chain (WSTS semiconductor billings, US electronics-store sales) give the same timing (step 5, G21, `graph_vs_step4_continuous_lag.csv`); and with one down-up cycle the correlation is flat over ~19–36 weeks, so the data do not pin the lag (P86). The ~2-quarter peak is the industry cycle at Nordic, not the Logitech -> Nordic order lag. No two-factor (Logitech + cycle) regression is fitted (L3).

**Lag, edge by edge** (`src/edge_lags.py`, decisions L4–L9, config `edge_lags:`): the chain Amazon / Ingram / TD Synnex --edge 1--> Logitech / GN --edge 2--> Nordic (ODM and Nordic's distributors folded into edge 2). Reasoning first: a prior per edge x brand computed from step 5's graph (`edge_priors.py`: flow-weighted, cut at the brand node, grade-widened low / high), on the order-signal basis (physical dwell + the buyers' planning delay, step 5 G25; decision L10), with the physical dwell alone shown next to it. Edge 1: channel-inventory partial adjustment on step 3's Logitech channel index, Andrews median-unbiased rho, mean lag T = -13 / ln(rho) weeks (`edge1_channel.py`). Edge 2: attribution-constrained fit with the slice size fixed (step 2 share x step 3 multiplier) and WSTS as the common-cycle control, destock + normal quarters, profile-likelihood set + block bootstrap (`edge2_constrained.py`). Posterior per edge = precision-weighted, or the prior when the data cannot place the lag (`edge_posterior.py`); route totals add the edges (Amazon direct, via Ingram / TD Synnex, flow-weighted). Outputs `outputs/edge_lags.csv`, `edge_lags_total.csv`, `edge_lags_edge1_fits.csv`, `edge_lags_edge2_fits.csv`; report section 3 (`edge_lags_report.py`); pitfalls P90 (small-sample AR bias), P91 (the constraint fixes size, not timing).

`src/step4_report.py` writes `outputs/step4_report.md` (called from step 7's `report.py`; reads step 2's attribution and step 5's continuous-lag table, no number typed in). Outputs: `outputs/xcorr_all.csv`, `xcorr_by_regime.csv`, `lag_analysis.json`, `xcorr.png`, `step4_report.md`. Decisions: `config/decisions.csv` (L1–L10).
